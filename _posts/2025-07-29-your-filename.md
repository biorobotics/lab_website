## Holodeck
![Holodeck_demo.gif]({{site.baseurl}}/img/posts/Holodeck_demo.gif)

{% include google-drive.html video="1F6HZutNI83FvObev3Zzm9o647Q4LfpR-/view" %}

Holodeck is a versatile emulation platform designed to simulate interactions between multiple physical objects in a free-floating, three-dimensional environment. These objects can be self-actuated, passively floating, or tumbling, and may operate in various domains, including on-orbit, underwater, and aerial environments.

At the heart of the Holodeck are four high-precision, six-degree-of-freedom (6-DOF) robotic manipulators, each equipped with a force-torque (F-T) sensor at the wrist. To overcome the limited workspace of each arm, they are mounted on actuated carriages with one linear degree of freedom. These rails allow each arm to translate up to six meters, significantly extending the reachable workspace and enabling the emulation of long-range trajectories.

The key innovation of the Holodeck lies in its programmability—both the environment and the objects within it can be precisely controlled, allowing for rich, repeatable experimentation across diverse physical scenarios.
